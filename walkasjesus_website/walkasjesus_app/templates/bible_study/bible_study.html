{% trans 'How the word links are made' %}

{% trans "For the King James Version and the Dutch Statenvertaling the link is exact: those editions were tagged word by word with Strong's numbers long ago, so we simply look the number up." %}

{% trans 'Modern revisions such as the Herziene Statenvertaling are matched against that older tagged edition; where the wording still agrees, the link carries over. Where it differs too much, no link is shown rather than a wrong one.' %}

{% trans 'For other English translations there is no tagging, so the link is estimated: every original word carries a short English rendering plus its dictionary meanings, and the sentence is matched in reading order. This is a best effort and can occasionally be off by a word.' %}
